Estimates per cohort.

Each row is a month of applications for proof of citizenship. IRCC mostly processes them oldest first: a sweep moves through one cohort at a time, while a slow trickle processes some files out of order.

The sweep takes about half of whoever is still waiting when it reaches a cohort. The rest are leftovers, which keep clearing at a few percent a month.

Estimates use the rates over the last 3 months of IRCC's published counts, which are rounded to the nearest 100. They update when IRCC refreshes its feed.
